# Code of Conduct

GPForum contributors are expected to keep the project rigorous, humane, and
useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise and respectful.
- Critique code, architecture, and evidence rather than people.
- Explain tradeoffs when rejecting or changing an idea.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Treat security, privacy, and moderation topics with care.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Dismissing security or privacy concerns without investigation.
- Derailing technical discussions with bad-faith repetition.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations.
