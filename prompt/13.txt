GPForum — Domain Model, Core Entities & Business Architecture Constitution

This document defines the mandatory domain architecture, business entity philosophy, aggregate boundaries, lifecycle rules, invariants, ownership semantics, revision strategies, and long-term domain consistency principles of GPForum.

This specification is foundational and mandatory.

⸻

Domain Philosophy

GPForum is:

* a distributed community platform;
* an event-driven social system;
* a long-lived collaborative environment.

The domain model MUST prioritize:

* explicit business meaning;
* bounded responsibility;
* auditability;
* scalability;
* operational sustainability;
* long-term coherence.

The domain model MUST avoid:

* accidental complexity;
* god entities;
* implicit relationships;
* uncontrolled cross-domain coupling.

⸻

Domain Architecture Philosophy

The domain layer MUST:

* remain infrastructure-independent;
* remain persistence-independent;
* remain transport-independent.

Business rules MUST live inside:

* domain services;
* application workflows;
* domain invariants.

The domain model MUST remain authoritative for:

* business semantics;
* ownership rules;
* moderation constraints;
* lifecycle rules.

⸻

Aggregate Philosophy

Aggregates MUST remain:

* bounded;
* explicit;
* consistency-aware.

Aggregates SHOULD:

* minimize locking scope;
* minimize transactional complexity;
* support distributed workflows.

The architecture MUST avoid:

* giant transactional aggregates;
* hidden cross-domain mutation.

⸻

* conversation containers;
* moderation scopes;
* realtime synchronization targets.

Threads MUST support:

Post Philosophy

Posts are:

* immutable historical artifacts;
* user-owned content entities;
* moderation targets.

Posts SHOULD prefer:

* append-oriented revision history.

Post editing SHOULD generate:

* revision records;
* audit trails;
* synchronization events.

⸻

Revision Philosophy

* immutable revisions.

Examples:

* post_revisions
* moderation_revisions
* policy_revisions

Historical reconstruction SHOULD remain possible.

* attachment ownership

Ownership MUST support:

⸻

Lifecycle Philosophy

All major entities SHOULD support:

* creation;
* moderation;
* archival;
* expiration where applicable.

Lifecycle state MUST remain:

* explicit;
* queryable;
* auditable.

⸻

Visibility Philosophy

Visibility MUST support:

* public visibility;
* restricted visibility;
* moderated visibility;
* quarantined visibility;
* deleted visibility.

Visibility rules MUST remain:

* authorization-aware;
* moderation-aware.

⸻

Soft Deletion Philosophy

Destructive deletion SHOULD be minimized.

Preferred approach:

* soft deletion;
* archival;
* reversible moderation.

Hard deletion SHOULD remain:

* exceptional;
* restricted;
* auditable.

⸻

Event Integration

All major domain workflows SHOULD emit:

* domain events;
* moderation events;
* synchronization events;
* audit events.

The domain model MUST remain:

* event-aware;
* replay-friendly.

⸻

Invariant Philosophy

Critical invariants MUST remain:

* explicit;
* testable;
* centralized.

Examples:

* locked threads reject replies;
* suspended users cannot post;
* quarantined content cannot render publicly.

Invariant enforcement MUST remain server-authoritative.

⸻

CQRS-lite Philosophy

The domain model SHOULD support:

* canonical write persistence;
* asynchronous projections;
* denormalized read models.

Read models MUST remain:

* rebuildable;
* eventually consistent where appropriate.

⸻

Domain Service Philosophy

Complex workflows SHOULD use:

* domain services;
* application workflows.

Entities SHOULD avoid:

* infrastructure coupling;
* orchestration complexity.

⸻

Long-Term Domain Goal

The GPForum domain architecture MUST remain:

* coherent;
* scalable;
* auditable;
* distributed-safe;
* operationally sustainable;
* understandable by future maintainers.

The domain model is the semantic foundation of the platform.

All future business logic and entities MUST comply with this constitution.
